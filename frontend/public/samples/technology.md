FICTIONAL — Lumen Apps (US): discovery workshop notes, engineering hiring

Lumen Apps is a 200-person startup hiring Software Engineers.

Process they want configured:
1. Application Review by the recruiter.
2. Recruiter Screen, 20 minutes.
3. Founder Chat: every finalist meets one of the co-founders.
4. Virtual Onsite Loop with three engineers.
5. Offer.

Roles: Recruiter; Engineering Manager (hiring manager); Interviewers.

Approval rules: offers above the compensation band need approval from the CTO.
