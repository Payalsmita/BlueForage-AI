FICTIONAL — Polar Wings (Canada): kickoff call notes, pilot hiring

Polar Wings is a new iCIMS customer hiring First Officers for bases in Calgary and Winnipeg.

What the customer described:
- Applications are reviewed by the recruiter for ATPL and flight hours.
- The recruiter does a short phone screen to confirm base preference.
- The recruiter also does a social media review of every candidate before the interview.
- Candidates then have a panel interview with the Chief Pilot.
- The candidate must show a valid Transport Canada Category 1 Medical.
- Then we make an offer.

Approvals: offers above the standard pay band need approval from the VP of Flight Operations.

Roles mentioned: Recruiter, Chief Pilot.
