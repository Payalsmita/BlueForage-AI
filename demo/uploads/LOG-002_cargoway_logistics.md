# FICTIONAL — Cargoway Logistics: Warehouse Associate Peak-Season Hiring

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Cargoway ramps up from 2,000 to 5,000 Warehouse Associates for the holiday peak.

## Hiring stages
1. **Online Application** – Includes a physical-requirements acknowledgement.
2. **Phone Screen** – Recruiter checks shift availability (also called Shift Preference Screening).
3. **Group Hiring Event** – Candidates tour the site and meet the Site Supervisor; replaces a one-to-one interview during peak.
4. **Background Check**
5. **Drug Screen**
6. **Offer**
7. **Forklift Certification**

## Roles
- **Site Supervisor** – is the hiring manager and runs the group hiring event.
- **Recruiter** – owns screening and offers.
- **Operations Manager** – approves requisitions.
- **Safety Coordinator** – owns forklift certification.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require Operations Manager approval.
- Peak-season sign-on bonuses require Operations Manager approval.
- Forklift work is blocked until the Safety Coordinator signs off certification.
