# FICTIONAL — HarbourLink Distribution (UK): Warehouse Operative Hiring

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

HarbourLink hires Warehouse Operatives for six UK distribution hubs.

## Hiring stages
1. **Online Application**
2. **Shift Preference Screening** – Recruiter confirms shift pattern and notice period.
3. **Site Supervisor Interview** – Includes a short pick-and-pack exercise.
4. **Right to Work Check** – Must be completed before the first shift.
5. **Background Check**
6. **Offer**
7. **Forklift Certification** – Counterbalance licence for operatives on powered equipment.

## Roles
- **Site Supervisor** – is the hiring manager.
- **Recruiter** – owns screening and the Right to Work check.
- **Operations Manager** – approves requisitions and pay above the standard rate.
- **Safety Coordinator** – signs off forklift certification.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require Operations Manager approval.
- Pay above the standard rate requires Operations Manager approval.
- No operative works a shift until the Right to Work check is complete.
