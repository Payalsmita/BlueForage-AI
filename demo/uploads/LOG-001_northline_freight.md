# FICTIONAL — Northline Freight: Warehouse Associate Hiring

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Northline Freight hires Warehouse Associates for 40 distribution centres on three shifts.

## Hiring stages
1. **Online Application** – Knockout questions on age (18+), shift availability and ability to lift 50 lb.
2. **Shift Preference Screening** – Recruiter confirms shift and start date by phone.
3. **Site Supervisor Interview**
4. **Drug Screen**
5. **Background Check**
6. **Offer**
7. **Forklift Certification** – Required before the associate operates powered equipment.

## Roles
- **Site Supervisor** – is the hiring manager.
- **Recruiter** – runs screening and schedules interviews.
- **Operations Manager** – approves requisitions and shift differentials.
- **Safety Coordinator** – signs off forklift certification.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require Operations Manager approval.
- Pay above the standard shift rate requires Operations Manager approval.
- An associate may not operate a forklift until the Safety Coordinator signs off certification.
