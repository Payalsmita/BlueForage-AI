# FICTIONAL — Aurora Airlines (Canada): Pilot Hiring Implementation Summary

*Synthetic sample. Approved configuration summary.*

Aurora hires First Officers across Canadian bases under Transport Canada rules.

Hiring stages configured:
1. Application Review — recruiter verifies ATPL or CPL with Multi-Engine IFR rating and flight hours.
2. Recruiter Phone Screen.
3. Written Technical Assessment — aviation knowledge and situational judgement.
4. Simulator Evaluation — conducted by a Simulator Check Airman.
5. Panel Interview — Director of Flight Operations and HR Business Partner.
6. Background Check — criminal record check and Transport Canada licence verification.
7. Medical Verification — valid Transport Canada Category 1 Medical.
8. Offer.

There is no pre-employment drug test stage in Canada for this customer.

Roles: Recruiter, Simulator Check Airman, Director of Flight Operations (hiring manager), HR Business Partner.

Approval rules:
- All requisitions require approval by the Director of Flight Operations and Finance.
- Offers above the standard pay band require approval from the VP of Flight Operations and the HR Director.
