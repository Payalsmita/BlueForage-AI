# FICTIONAL — Thamesbridge NHS Trust: Registered Nurse Recruitment (UK)

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Thamesbridge NHS Trust recruits Band 5 Registered Nurses.

## Hiring stages
1. **Application Review** – Shortlisting against the person specification.
2. **Values-Based Interview**
3. **Clinical Scenario Assessment**
4. **NMC Registration Check**
5. **Enhanced DBS Check**
6. **Occupational Health Clearance**
7. **Reference Check**
8. **Offer** – Conditional offer issued after interview; unconditional after checks.

## Roles
- **Recruiter** – owns pre-employment checks.
- **Ward Manager** – is the hiring manager.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require approval from the Divisional Director and Finance.
- Recruitment and retention premiums require approval from the Director of Nursing.
