# FICTIONAL — Ledgerline Bank: Advisor Recruiting Configuration Notes

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Ledgerline Bank recruits Financial Advisors for in-branch wealth teams.

### Stage configuration
| Order | Stage | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resume Screen | Recruiter |
| 2 | Recruiter Screen | Recruiter |
| 3 | Branch Manager Interview | Branch Manager |
| 4 | Case Study Presentation | Branch Manager |
| 5 | Background & Credit Check | Compliance Officer |
| 6 | Fingerprinting | HR Business Partner |
| 7 | FINRA Registration Check | Compliance Officer |
| 8 | Offer | HR Business Partner |

### Roles
- Recruiter: screens resumes.
- Branch Manager: acts as hiring manager.
- Compliance Officer: owns registration and background checks.
- HR Business Partner: owns the offer.

### Approvals
- Requisitions require approval from the Regional Director and Finance.
- Offers above the standard pay band need approval from the Regional Director and the HR Director.
- Candidates with Form U4 disclosures need Chief Compliance Officer approval.
