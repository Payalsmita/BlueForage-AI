# FICTIONAL — Harborstone Wealth: Financial Advisor Hiring Implementation

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Harborstone Wealth hires licensed Financial Advisors for its branch network.

## Hiring stages
1. **Application Review** – Recruiter checks licenses held and years of client-facing experience.
2. **Recruiter Phone Screen**
3. **Hiring Manager Interview** – Interview with the Branch Manager.
4. **Client Role-Play Exercise** – Candidate runs a mock client review meeting.
5. **Panel Interview** – Branch Manager, Regional Director, and HR Business Partner.
6. **Licensing Verification** – FINRA Series 7 and Series 66 verified through BrokerCheck.
7. **Background Check** – Includes Form U4 disclosure review and a credit check.
8. **Fingerprinting** – Fingerprints submitted to FINRA.
9. **Offer**

## Roles
- **Recruiter** – screens applicants.
- **Branch Manager** – is the hiring manager.
- **Regional Director** – leads the panel interview.
- **Compliance Officer** – owns licensing and background review.
- **HR Business Partner** – prepares the offer.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require approval from the Regional Director and Finance.
- Offers above the standard pay band require approval from the Regional Director and the HR Director.
- Any draw or compensation guarantee requires approval from Finance and Compliance.
- Candidates with disclosures on Form U4 require approval from the Chief Compliance Officer.
