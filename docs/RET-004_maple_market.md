# FICTIONAL — Maple Market: Store Associate Hiring Summary (Canada)

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Maple Market hires Store Associates for grocery stores in Ontario and Quebec.

## Hiring stages
1. **Online Application** – Bilingual application form (English and French).
2. **Store Manager Interview**
3. **Reference Check**
4. **Offer**

## Roles
- **Store Manager** – is the hiring manager and owns the offer.
- **District Manager** – approves requisitions.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require District Manager approval.
- Starting pay above the standard rate requires District Manager approval.

Note: Maple Market does not use an assessment or a background check for Store Associates.
