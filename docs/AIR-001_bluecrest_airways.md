# FICTIONAL — Bluecrest Airways: First Officer Hiring Implementation

*Implementation summary, approved by customer project lead. Synthetic sample data.*

## Workflow overview
Bluecrest hires First Officers for its A320 fleet. Every requisition must be approved by the Director of Flight Operations and Finance before it is posted.

## Hiring stages
1. **Application Review** – The recruiter checks minimum flight hours (1,500 total, 500 multi-engine) and ATP certificate.
2. **Recruiter Phone Screen** – 20-minute call covering availability, base preference, and type ratings.
3. **Written Technical Assessment** – Online systems and aviation knowledge test; pass mark 80%.
4. **Simulator Evaluation** – A Simulator Check Airman runs a 2-hour A320 sim session.
5. **Panel Interview** – Chief Pilot and an HR Business Partner conduct a structured panel interview.
6. **Background Check** – Includes the FAA PRIA records request and a 10-year employment history verification.
7. **Medical Verification** – Candidate must hold a valid FAA First-Class Medical Certificate.
8. **Pre-Employment Drug Test** – DOT-compliant drug test required before offer acceptance.
9. **Offer** – Offer letter generated from the pilot pay scale.

## Roles
- **Recruiter** – owns application review and phone screen, schedules all later steps.
- **Simulator Check Airman** – conducts and scores the simulator evaluation.
- **Chief Pilot** – leads panel interview and makes the hire recommendation.
- **HR Business Partner** – co-interviews and prepares the offer.

## Approval rules
- Any offer above the standard First Officer pay band requires approval from the VP of Flight Operations and the HR Director.
- Sign-on bonuses must be approved by Finance.
