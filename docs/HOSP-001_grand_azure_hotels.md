# FICTIONAL — Grand Azure Hotels: Front Desk Agent Hiring

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Grand Azure Hotels hires Front Desk Agents for full-service hotels.

## Hiring stages
1. **Application Review**
2. **Recruiter Phone Screen**
3. **Front Office Manager Interview**
4. **Guest Scenario Role-Play** – Candidate handles a mock guest complaint at check-in.
5. **Background Check**
6. **Offer**

## Roles
- **Recruiter** – screens.
- **Front Office Manager** – is the hiring manager.
- **HR Coordinator** – owns checks and offers.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require approval from the General Manager and Finance.
- Starting pay above the standard rate requires General Manager approval.
