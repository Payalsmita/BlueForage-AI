# FICTIONAL — Pinnacle Regional: Technical Requirements Extract (Pilot Hiring)

*Synthetic sample. Section 4 of the approved TRS.*

4.1 Scope: First Officer hiring for the E175 fleet, US bases only.

4.2 Requisition approval: Requisitions require approval from the Director of Flight Operations and Finance.

4.3 Workflow stages (in order):
a. Application Review — automated knockout questions for ATP and 1,500 hours, then recruiter review.
b. Recruiter Phone Screen.
c. Simulator Evaluation — E175 sim session run by a Simulator Check Airman.
d. Panel Interview — Chief Pilot, a line Captain, and HR Business Partner.
e. Background Check — PRIA records and criminal history.
f. Medical Verification — FAA First-Class Medical Certificate on file.
g. Pre-Employment Drug Test — DOT drug test.
h. Offer.

Note: Pinnacle does not run a written knowledge test; the simulator evaluation replaces it.

4.4 Roles: Recruiter; Simulator Check Airman; Chief Pilot; HR Business Partner.

4.5 Approval rules:
- Offers above the standard pay band require approval from the VP of Flight Operations and the HR Director.
- Sign-on bonuses require Finance approval.
- Credit for prior experience toward seniority must be approved by the Chief Pilot.
