# FICTIONAL — Brightpath Software: Implementation Workshop Notes (Engineering Hiring)

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Attendees: Brightpath TA lead, Director of Engineering, iCIMS consultant.

Agreed hiring workflow:
- Step 1, Application Review: recruiter checks degree, experience, and notice period.
- Step 2, Recruiter Phone Screen: confirm notice period and expected CTC.
- Step 3, Online Coding Test: timed test on the assessment vendor platform.
- Step 4, Technical Interview Round 1: data structures and coding.
- Step 5, Technical Interview Round 2: system design.
- Step 6, Hiring Manager Interview.
- Step 7, HR Interview.
- Step 8, Background Verification: education and previous employment verified by a vendor.
- Step 9, Offer.

Decision: Brightpath does NOT use a take-home project.

Decision: offer letters must state the notice-period buyout if applicable.

Roles confirmed: Recruiter; Hiring Manager (makes the hire decision); Interviewers; HR Business Partner (runs the HR interview and offer).

Approval rules agreed:
- Requisitions require approval from the VP of Engineering and Finance.
- Offers above the compensation band require approval from the VP of Engineering and the HR Head.
- Joining bonuses require Finance approval.
