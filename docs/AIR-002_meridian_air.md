# FICTIONAL — Meridian Air: Pilot Recruitment Configuration Notes

*Synthetic sample. Consultant handover notes after go-live.*

Meridian recruits pilots (First Officer and Direct-Entry Captain) for regional and mainline bases.

Requisitions are opened by the Fleet Captain and require sign-off from the Director of Flight Operations and Finance.

### Stage configuration
| Order | Stage | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resume Screening | Recruiter |
| 2 | HR Screen | Recruiter |
| 3 | Knowledge Test | Recruiter (online vendor) |
| 4 | Sim Check | Simulator Check Airman |
| 5 | Captain Interview | Fleet Captain + HR Business Partner |
| 6 | Background & PRIA Check | HR Business Partner |
| 7 | First-Class Medical Verification | HR Business Partner |
| 8 | DOT Drug Screen | HR Business Partner |
| 9 | Offer | HR Business Partner |

### Roles
- Recruiter: screens resumes against minimums and runs the HR Screen.
- Fleet Captain: acts as hiring manager and leads the Captain Interview.
- Simulator Check Airman: runs the Sim Check and records a pass/fail score.
- HR Business Partner: owns checks and offer.

### Approvals
- Offers above the standard pay band need approval from the VP Flight Operations and the HR Director.
- Credit for prior experience toward seniority must be approved by the Chief Pilot.
