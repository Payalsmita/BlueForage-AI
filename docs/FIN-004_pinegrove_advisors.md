# FICTIONAL — Pinegrove Advisors: Financial Advisor Hiring Summary

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Pinegrove Advisors is an independent advisory firm hiring Financial Advisors.

## Hiring stages
1. **Application Review**
2. **Recruiter Phone Screen**
3. **Hiring Manager Interview**
4. **Client Role-Play Exercise** – Candidate presents a portfolio review to a mock client.
5. **Licensing Verification** – Series 7 and 66 checked on BrokerCheck.
6. **Background Check**
7. **Offer**

## Roles
- **Recruiter** – screens applicants.
- **Branch Manager** – is the hiring manager and owns the offer.
- **Compliance Officer** – verifies licenses and runs background checks.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require approval from the Regional Director and Finance.
- Any draw or compensation guarantee requires approval from Finance and Compliance.

Note: Pinegrove does not run a panel interview.
