# FICTIONAL — Ironvale Motors: Production Technician Hiring Implementation

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Ironvale Motors hires Production Technicians for its assembly plants on three shifts.

## Hiring stages
1. **Application Review**
2. **Recruiter Phone Screen** – Confirms shift availability.
3. **Mechanical Aptitude Test** – 30-minute test on tools, measurement, and mechanical reasoning.
4. **Hands-On Skills Demonstration** – Candidate completes a timed assembly task on the plant floor.
5. **Production Supervisor Interview**
6. **Background Check**
7. **Drug Screen**
8. **Pre-Employment Physical** – Includes a lifting test.
9. **Offer**

## Roles
- **Recruiter** – screens and schedules.
- **Production Supervisor** – is the hiring manager.
- **Plant HR Manager** – owns checks and offers.
- **Safety Coordinator** – owns the physical.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require approval from the Plant Manager and Finance.
- Shift premiums above policy require Plant HR Manager approval.
- Offers for union-represented positions must be approved by Labor Relations.
