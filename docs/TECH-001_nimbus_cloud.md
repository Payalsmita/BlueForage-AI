# FICTIONAL — Nimbus Cloud: Software Engineer Hiring Implementation

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Nimbus Cloud hires backend and full-stack Software Engineers (L3-L5) for remote and hub roles.

## Hiring stages
1. **Application Review** – The recruiter checks years of experience and core language skills.
2. **Recruiter Phone Screen** – 30-minute call on motivation, level, and compensation expectations.
3. **Online Coding Assessment** – 90-minute timed assessment with two algorithm problems.
4. **Technical Phone Interview** – Live coding with a senior engineer.
5. **Virtual Onsite Loop** – Four interviews: two coding, one system design, one behavioral.
6. **Hiring Committee Review** – A hiring committee reviews all feedback and decides hire or no hire.
7. **Reference Check** – Two professional references.
8. **Background Check** – Employment and criminal history verification.
9. **Offer** – Offer generated from the engineering compensation bands.

## Roles
- **Recruiter** – owns screening and scheduling.
- **Engineering Manager** – is the hiring manager for the requisition.
- **Interviewers** – trained engineers who run technical interviews.
- **Hiring Committee** – makes the final hire decision.
- **Compensation Partner** – builds the offer.

## Approval rules
- Every requisition requires approval from the VP of Engineering and Finance.
- Offers above the compensation band require approval from the VP of Engineering and the Compensation team.
- Equity grants above guideline require CFO approval.
