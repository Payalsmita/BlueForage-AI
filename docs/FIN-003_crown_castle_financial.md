- Step 5, Panel Interview.

- Step 8, Offer.

Decision: fingerprinting is not used in the UK process.

Roles confirmed: Recruiter; Hiring Manager (the Regional Manager); Compliance Officer (owns regulatory references); HR Business Partner.

Approval rules agreed:
- Offers above the standard pay band require approval from the Regional Director and the HR Director.
