# FICTIONAL — Coastal Jet: Implementation Workshop Notes (Pilot Hiring)

*Synthetic meeting notes, workshop 3 of 4. Decisions confirmed by customer.*

Attendees: Coastal Jet Talent Acquisition lead, Chief Pilot, iCIMS consultant.

Decision: Coastal Jet does NOT use a simulator evaluation; they rely on the type-rating check done during training instead.

Agreed pilot hiring workflow:
- Step 1, Application Review: recruiter checks ATP certificate and flight hours.
- Step 2, Recruiter Phone Screen to confirm base and start date.
- Step 3, Written Technical Assessment taken online.
- Step 4, Panel Interview with the Chief Pilot and HR Business Partner.
- Step 5, Background Check including PRIA records.
- Step 6, Pre-Employment Drug Test (DOT).
- Step 7, Offer.

Decision: medical certificate verification happens after hire during onboarding, not as a hiring stage.

Roles confirmed: Recruiter, Chief Pilot (hiring decision maker), HR Business Partner.

Approval rules agreed:
- Every requisition needs approval from the Director of Flight Operations and Finance before posting.
- Any offer above the standard pay band requires approval from the VP of Flight Operations and the HR Director.
- Sign-on bonuses require Finance approval.
