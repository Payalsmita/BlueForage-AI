# FICTIONAL — Freshmart Grocers: Store Associate High-Volume Hiring

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Freshmart hires part-time and full-time Store Associates for 300 grocery stores.

## Hiring stages
1. **Online Application** – Knockout questions on age (18+) and weekend availability.
2. **Situational Judgement Assessment** – 15-minute customer service assessment.
3. **Store Manager Interview**
4. **Background Check**
5. **Offer**
6. **I-9 Verification** – Completed on or before the first day.

## Roles
- **Store Manager** – is the hiring manager.
- **Assistant Store Manager** – can interview when the Store Manager is unavailable.
- **District Manager** – approves requisitions and pay exceptions.
- **HR Coordinator** – owns checks and paperwork.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require District Manager approval.
- Starting pay above the standard rate requires District Manager approval.
- Rehiring a former employee requires the HR Coordinator to confirm rehire eligibility.
