# FICTIONAL — Autoforge: Technician Recruiting Design (Germany)

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Autoforge hires Mechatronics Technicians for its Stuttgart plant.

## Hiring stages
1. **Application Review** – Recruiter checks vocational qualification (Ausbildung).
2. **Recruiter Screen**
3. **Technical Skills Test**
4. **Supervisor Interview**
5. **Occupational Medical Exam**
6. **Offer**

## Roles
- **Recruiter** – screens applicants.
- **Production Supervisor** – is the hiring manager.
- **Plant HR Manager** – owns the offer.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require approval from the Plant Manager and Finance.
- Every hire requires Works Council approval before the offer is sent.
