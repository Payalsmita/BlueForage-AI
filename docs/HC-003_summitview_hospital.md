Summitview Hospital hires Registered Nurses for inpatient units.

### Stage configuration
| Order | Stage | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Application Review | Recruiter |
| 2 | Recruiter Phone Screen | Recruiter |
| 3 | Nurse Manager Interview | Nurse Manager |
| 4 | Peer Interview | Nurse Manager |
| 5 | License Verification | HR Generalist |
| 6 | Background Check | HR Generalist |
| 7 | Drug Screen | HR Generalist |
| 8 | Occupational Health Screening | HR Generalist |
| 9 | Offer | HR Generalist |

### Roles
- Recruiter: screens applicants.
- Nurse Manager: is the hiring manager.
- HR Generalist: owns checks and offers.

### Approvals
- Requisitions require approval from the Unit Director and Finance.
- Shift differentials above policy require Chief Nursing Officer approval.
