# FICTIONAL — Summit Air (UK): Pilot Recruitment Workflow

*Synthetic sample. Implementation design document, signed off.*

Summit Air recruits Second Officers and First Officers for UK bases.

Workflow:
- Application Review: recruiter checks UK CAA licence and hours.
- Online Knowledge Test: written technical assessment.
- Simulator Evaluation: run by a Simulator Check Airman (TRE).
- Panel Interview: Chief Pilot and HR Business Partner.
- Background Check: CAA licence verification and five-year referencing.
- Medical Verification: UK CAA Class 1 Medical.
- Offer.

Summit Air skips a recruiter phone screen; candidates go straight from application review to the online test.

Roles: Recruiter; Simulator Check Airman; Chief Pilot; HR Business Partner.

Approvals:
- Requisitions require approval from the Director of Flight Operations.
- Sign-on bonuses require Finance approval.
