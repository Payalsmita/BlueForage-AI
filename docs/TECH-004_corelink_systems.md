# FICTIONAL — Corelink Systems: Software Engineer Recruiting Design (Germany)

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Corelink Systems hires Software Engineers in Berlin and Munich.

## Hiring stages
1. **Application Review** – Recruiter reviews CV and work permit status.
2. **Recruiter Screen** – 30-minute video call.
3. **Pair Programming Session** – 90-minute pair programming session that replaces a coding test.
4. **System Design Interview**
5. **Team Fit Interview** – Conversation with the Engineering Manager and a future teammate.
6. **Reference Check**
7. **Offer**

## Roles
- **Recruiter** – owns screening and scheduling.
- **Engineering Manager** – is the hiring manager.
- **Interviewers** – run the pair programming and design interviews.

## Approval rules
- Requisitions require approval from the VP of Engineering and Finance.
- Offers above the compensation band require approval from the VP of Engineering and the Compensation team.
- Every hire must be notified to the Works Council before the offer is sent.

Note: Background checks are not performed for this role in Germany.
