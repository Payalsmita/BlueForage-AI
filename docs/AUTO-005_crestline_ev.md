# FICTIONAL — Crestline EV: Battery Plant Technician Configuration

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Crestline EV hires Production Technicians for its battery plant.

### Stage configuration
| Order | Stage | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Application Review | Recruiter |
| 2 | Recruiter Phone Screen | Recruiter |
| 3 | Mechanical Aptitude Test | Recruiter |
| 4 | Hands-On Skills Demonstration | Production Supervisor |
| 5 | Supervisor Interview | Production Supervisor |
| 6 | Background Check | Plant HR Manager |
| 7 | Drug Screen | Plant HR Manager |
| 8 | Pre-Employment Physical | Safety Coordinator |
| 9 | Offer | Plant HR Manager |

### Roles
- Recruiter: screens and schedules.
- Production Supervisor: is the hiring manager.
- Plant HR Manager: owns offers.
- Safety Coordinator: owns the physical.

### Approvals
- Requisitions require approval from the Plant Manager and Finance.
- Shift premiums above policy require Plant HR Manager approval.
