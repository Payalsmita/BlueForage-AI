# FICTIONAL — Northgate Securities: Investment Advisor Recruiting Configuration (Canada)

*Synthetic sample data. Approved by customer project lead.*

Northgate Securities hires Investment Advisors across Canadian branches.

### Stage configuration
| Order | Stage | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Application Review | Recruiter |
| 2 | Recruiter Screen | Recruiter |
| 3 | Branch Manager Interview | Branch Manager |
| 4 | Panel Interview | Regional Director |
| 5 | CIRO Registration Verification | Compliance Officer |
| 6 | Criminal Record and Credit Check | Compliance Officer |
| 7 | Offer | HR Business Partner |

### Roles
- Recruiter: screens applicants.
- Branch Manager: is the hiring manager.
- Regional Director: chairs the panel.
- Compliance Officer: owns registration checks.
- HR Business Partner: prepares the offer.

### Approvals
- Requisitions require approval from the Regional Director and Finance.
- Offers above the standard pay band require approval from the Regional Director and the HR Director.
- Candidates with regulatory disclosures require Chief Compliance Officer approval.
